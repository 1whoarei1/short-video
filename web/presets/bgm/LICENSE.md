# Original BGM preset collection — CC0 1.0

The five compositions, their newly authored note patterns, generated instruments,
rendered PCM recordings and composition source were created for this project's
public offline BGM presets on 2026-10-06. No existing melody, private video score,
third-party sample, stock audio, soundfont, online model or paid service was used.
All oscillator, filtered-noise and room-reflection sounds are synthesized locally
by `scripts/compose_bgm_presets.py`. NumPy and FFmpeg are computational tools,
not sources of audio recordings.
