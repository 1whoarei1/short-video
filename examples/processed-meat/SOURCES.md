## Medical references

- WHO. Cancer: Carcinogenicity of the consumption of red meat and processed meat. Updated 23 September 2026. https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/cancer-carcinogenicity-of-the-consumption-of-red-meat-and-processed-meat/
- IARC. Q&A on the carcinogenicity of the consumption of red meat and processed meat (2015), pp. 2–3. https://www.iarc.who.int/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/Monographs-QA_Vol114.pdf
- IARC. Press release no. 240, 26 October 2015. https://www.iarc.who.int/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/pr240_E.pdf

The 50 g / 18% estimate is explicitly attributed to the 2015 evaluation's referenced analysis of ten studies. It describes relative colorectal-cancer risk associated with daily intake differences, not an individual's probability, an 18-percentage-point increase, or the consequence of one meal. The current WHO Q&A omits that estimate; this project does not infer a reason for the omission.
