# 有机光感 · 叶隙之间

苔绿留白、透光叶片与缓慢光斑；用呼吸式尺度和光照相位表现材料、环境和感受。

## 直接运行

打开 `preview.html`（可离线 file://），或从仓库 HTTP 服务打开。原生 1280×720，8 秒无声动画。
冻结代表帧：`preview.html?t=3.5&controls=0`。控制台：`render(3.5)`；任意顺序 seek 可复现。
本页是素材与动效示范，不是必须沿用的镜头模板、脚本或八秒时长要求。

## 可拆出来用的材料

- `assets/translucent-leaf.svg`：原创、可编辑 SVG
- `assets/contour-stone.svg`：原创、可编辑 SVG

- `assets/poster.svg`：便携矢量封面，不依赖其他文件
- `styles.css`：材质、排版和构图；`motion.js`：以秒为输入的动效函数
- `../shared/`：纹理、缓动、播放器，可共用或拆散

## 自由改编

Keep a large quiet field for the message. Layer translucent shapes and let a slow light pass reveal the material. Reuse the stone for tactile product or wellness stories without making health claims.

- **透光材质**：Replace the leaf with authorized material imagery and keep one slowly moving light wash. Disable blend effects when they reduce contrast.
- **等高线静物**：Promote contour-stone.svg to the hero, use a muted canvas and draw its concentric paths sequentially for a tactile explanation.

### 动效参考

0–1.9s: text and material emerge; 2.2–3.4s: palette labels appear. Light and leaf motion remain slow and non-flashing.

### 竖屏改编

Place the leaf above or beside a shorter title. Keep translucent layers behind, rather than crossing, body text; reserve a quiet lower area for captions.

### 内容边界

Do not claim this abstract leaf is a verified botanical species. Soft motion still needs sufficient text contrast and readable dwell time.
